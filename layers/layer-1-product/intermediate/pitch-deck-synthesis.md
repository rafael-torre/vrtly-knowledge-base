# Pitch Deck Synthesis — Vrtly Q1 2025

Source: 40-slide investor pitch deck.

---

## Product Vision

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`*

Vrtly is a **point-of-care (POC) advertising platform for medical aesthetics**. It connects aesthetic brands (advertisers) with medical practices (venues) via an AI-powered ad network delivered through Smart TV apps installed on practice waiting room and treatment room screens.

The core vision: enable brands to speak directly to consumers at the most critical moment of the buying journey — while they are physically inside a practice — and give practices a free, modern alternative to print and USB-stick-based advertising.

Self-described as the **"All-in-One Media Network"** for medical aesthetics.

---

## Problem Space

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`*

**The in-practice advertising problem (from brand perspective):**

- Brands have no real-time access to consumers at the POC, when it really matters
- Brands waste millions on clunky POC advertising methods (print, USB sticks)
- Brands have no visibility into what marketing is being used in practices or what is proving effective

**Supporting market context (appendix slide 40):**

- Over two-thirds of medical aesthetics industry revenue (>$55B annually) comes from existing/repeat consumers
- 70% of consumers are interested in new procedures, but 7 in 10 do not have a specific brand in mind at point of care
- With recent privacy changes, reaching this audience via digital marketing channels is increasingly difficult
- 80% of practices already have TVs at POC; over 250 brands compete for consumer attention; content is currently distributed manually by sales reps via USB sticks

Sources: AmSpa 2023, Straits Research 2024, Boston Consulting Group 2024, McKinsey 2024, Digiday 2024, Vrtly Market Research 2024

---

## Solution

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`*

Instantly stream brand ads directly into practices via Smart TV apps. Three core value propositions:

1. Speak directly to consumers at the POC to drive cross-sell revenue
2. Instant distribution to thousands of practices and millions of consumers
3. Real-time visibility into where ads play and how consumers engage

## Platform Architecture (How It Works)

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`, `final/features/`*

Vrtly operates as a **two-sided marketplace** with a central exchange:


| Component                                    | Side                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vrtly Ad Manager**                         | Demand-side (Brands)    | Advertisers upload content, plan and launch ad campaigns, view real-time insights. Also described as: launch, track, optimize campaigns with real-time proof-of-play, gold-standard analytics, QR/text engagement |
| **Vrtly Ad Exchange**                        | Platform (matching)     | AI-powered optimization and programmatic matching. Ensures brand ads reach the right audience. Gold-standard video ad serving                                                                                     |
| **Vrtly Practice Manager / Practice Portal** | Supply-side (Practices) | Smart TV app — practices select brands, activate screens. Practices engage patients, showcase custom content, and boost revenue with free or paid options                                                         |

### Live

#### Free Screens (Practice Portal)

- Free for practices — brand-backed
- Practices choose which brands to feature (select ≥2 brands, opt out of unwanted ones)
- Unlimited free screens
- Access to brand content library
- Ability to add own images and videos, schedule promos and events
- Custom content up to 25%

#### Vrtly Pro (Paid Screens)

- Full control over marketing content — 100% share of voice on paid screens
- Pricing:
  - Pro Lite: 2 screens — $99/mo
  - Pro Plus: 5 screens — $199/mo (save 20%)
  - Pro Max: 10 screens — $299/mo (save 40%, $29.90/mo per additional screen)
- Pro use cases: Consultation Tool, Open House Tool, Pricing Boards, Full Marketing Control

#### Vrtly Marketplace (2025 target)

Four service pillars to deepen practice relationships:

- **Group Purchasing**: Bulk product discounts, supply chain management
- **Business Services**: Accounting, legal, HR for practices
- **Marketing Services**: Content creation, local marketing, social media

- **Hyper-Targeted POC Ads**: CRM/EMR integration for better POC and cross-channel marketing
- **Live Broadcast & Social**: Live brand broadcasts and integrations with live social platforms

#### Next-Gen Automation

- **Marketing Automation Engine**: Optimized in-practice marketing, automated new patient campaigns on Google & Meta, IoT campaigns via Bluetooth sensor
- **AI Agent Brand Reps**: Product inquiry and education, logistics and ordering, support and follow-ups

#### Vrtly Retail Media Network

Four interconnected data and commerce components:

- **Vrtly Marketing Engine**: Automatically launch ad campaigns across Vrtly Media Network and 3P ad networks (Meta, Google)
- **Vrtly DMP & DSP (Ad Intelligence)**: AI-driven audience segmentation and modeling from purchase data
- **Vrtly Ads EMR (1P Customer Data)**: Collect purchase behavior data to feed RMN ecosystem and optimize ad effectiveness
- **Vrtly Commerce Engine (1P Practice Data)**: One-stop-shop for providers to digitally finance and purchase all aesthetics products

## Target Users

→ *maps to: `final/user-personas.md`*

Two distinct user sides:

**Brands (Advertisers — demand side)**

- Medical aesthetics brands (e.g., Hydrafacial, Allergan Aesthetics / Allē, Restylane, Marini, Motus AZ+, SmartXide, Venus Versa, Botox, Juvederm, Dysport, Revance, CoolSculpting)
- Goal: reach consumers at the POC, measure ad effectiveness, drive cross-sell revenue
- Pain: no real-time POC access, no visibility into ad placement or effectiveness, wasted spend on outdated methods

**Practices (Publishers — supply side)**

- Medical aesthetic practices (medspa, dermatology, plastic surgery)
- Goal: engage patients in waiting and treatment rooms, generate ancillary revenue, control what is advertised in their space
- Pain: outdated advertising materials (print, USB sticks), manual brand rep visits, no modern digital signage without hardware cost
- Onboarding supports practice size: Small (<1,200 sq ft), Medium (<2,400 sq ft), Large (>2,400 sq ft)

**Consumers (End audience — not a direct user of the platform)**

- Patients in aesthetics practices
- 70% interested in new procedures; 7 in 10 without a brand preference at time of visit
- Existing/repeat consumers drive 2/3 of industry revenue — the high-value target for cross-sell

## Key Terms

→ *maps to: `final/domain-glossary.md`*


| Term                       | Definition (from deck context)                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| POC / Point-of-Care        | The physical location where a patient receives or considers a medical aesthetics treatment — the most critical moment in the buying journey  |
| Practice                   | A medical aesthetics business (medspa, dermatology clinic, plastic surgery office) that hosts screens and delivers brand content to patients |
| Brand                      | An aesthetics product company (e.g., Hydrafacial, Allergan, Restylane) that advertises through Vrtly                                         |
| Free Screens               | Practice screens funded by brand advertising; 80% SOV available to brands; free for practices                                                |
| Pro Screens                | Paid screens with 100% SOV control for the practice; $99+/mo                                                                                 |
| SOV (Share of Voice)       | The percentage of screen time allocated to a given advertiser or content type                                                                |
| CPM                        | Cost per thousand impressions — $30 CPM is the pricing basis for brand ad campaigns                                                          |
| Ad Exchange                | Vrtly's central matching layer; AI-powered programmatic matching of brands to practices                                                      |
| DSP (Demand-Side Platform) | Vrtly Ad Manager — the brand-facing interface for planning and running campaigns                                                             |
| SSP (Supply-Side Platform) | Vrtly Practice Portal — the practice-facing interface for selecting brands and activating screens                                            |
| DMP                        | Data Management Platform — Vrtly's audience data and segmentation capability (roadmap)                                                       |
| EMR                        | Electronic Medical Record — referenced in context of integrating purchase behavior data (Vrtly Ads EMR)                                      |
| RMN                        | Retail Media Network — Vrtly's evolving commerce and data ecosystem                                                                          |
| CSAT                       | Customer Satisfaction score — Vrtly reports >95                                                                                              |
| Cross-sell                 | Selling additional treatments or products to existing patients; the primary value proposition for brands advertising at POC                  |
