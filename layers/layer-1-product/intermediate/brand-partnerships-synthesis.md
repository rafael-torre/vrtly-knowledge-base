# Brand Partnerships Synthesis — Vrtly V1 (2026)

Source: 28-slide brand partnership sales deck. Dated 2026, Vrtly Inc. Audience: prospective brand/advertiser partners. Purpose: sell brands on joining the Vrtly ad network. Treated as a primary product and go-to-market input, not a final document.

---

## Value Proposition for Brands

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`*

Taglines used:
- **"The Point of Care is the Point of Decision"**
- **"Bridging the Blind Spot for Brands in Cash-Pay Healthcare"**
- **"Smart Screens, Smarter Practice."**

Core pitch: Brands spend heavily on digital advertising (Meta, Google) to build awareness, but lose the consumer at the moment that matters most — when they are physically inside a clinic. Vrtly closes this loop by delivering brand content at the point of care, where 80% of decisions happen.

Three things Vrtly claims to give brands that nothing else does:
1. **Presence at decision** — reach patients during 45–90 min of dwell time at peak receptivity
2. **Measurable performance** — real-time analytics on content exposure, engagement, and conversion (previously invisible)
3. **No reps required** — brands run the channel centrally; content is deployed and updated without physical distribution

## Problem Space (Brand Perspective)

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`*

**The funnel leaks at the POC:**

- 65% of new treatment conversations are prompted at the POC — but most brands miss this moment
- Without a POC prompt, upstream digital spend fails to convert; reinforcement is absent
- The POC is invisible to brands: no visibility, no attribution, no way to improve performance

**Current POC marketing is broken in four ways (slide 4):**

| Problem | Description |
|---|---|
| **Waste** | Up to 40% of materials never reach patients |
| **Manual** | Every update requires physical effort — nothing is automated, measurable, or repeatable |
| **Outdated** | Most printed materials are obsolete before they are replaced |
| **Blind** | No visibility, attribution, or way to improve performance |

**Supporting market data:**
- 80% of decisions happen in-clinic
- 45–90 min average dwell time per visit
- Peak receptivity occurs during the consultation window
- Strong cross-sell moment: patients are already in the decision mindset

## How the Platform Works (Brand-Facing View)

→ *maps to: `final/product-brief.md`, `final/features/`*

Vrtly operates as a **shared in-practice platform** that lives inside the clinic where decisions happen:

- **Brands run it centrally** — one place for content, updates, and messaging; no sales reps, no manual distribution
- **Clinics stay in control** — practices choose which brands they show; the system automates the rest
- **Engagement becomes visible, measurable, and continuous**

**Playlist Dynamics (how brand content is delivered on-screen):**

- Content allocation per screen: **80% brand content** (shared across an avg of 22 brands per playlist) / **20% practice content** (before/after photos, custom messaging)
- **Platform Brands** receive 2× more ad space than non-platform brands
- **Content Refresh Bonus**: brands that update content regularly receive increased play frequency
- Brands active on a paid campaign receive priority SOV allocation

**Ads Mode vs. organic presence:**
- Slide 7 shows "Ads Mode" as a distinct content delivery state in the practice — brand logos shown: Mentor, Galderma, Dysport, Sculptra, Restylane

### 4. Vrtly Success Suite — Device Sale Package (Live)

A bundled package sold to practices but funded/co-sold through brand partners. $5,000/yr per practice.

Includes:
- Set up
- 3 screens (TV or tablets) — described as saving practices $4,500
- Monthly concierge promo automation (Studio supported)
- Priority Playlists (brand controls % SOV)
- 50% SOV reserved for the sponsoring brand(s)
- Custom and editable Info Packs and Consults (Studio supported)

This is a **device-led partnership model**: brands fund or subsidize screen installations in target clinics to secure guaranteed SOV.

## Key Terms

→ *maps to: `final/domain-glossary.md`*

| Term | Definition (from deck context) |
|---|---|
| POC / Point of Care | Physical location inside a clinic where treatment decisions happen; the primary targeting context for Vrtly |
| Platform Brand | A brand that has paid for the Vrtly Ad Manager platform subscription; receives 2× more ad space than non-platform brands |
| SOV (Share of Voice) | Percentage of screen time allocated to a given brand's content |
| CPM (Cost Per Mille) | Cost per 1,000 impressions; Vrtly charges $20 CPM for 30s campaign ads |
| SmartPlaylist | An AI-curated screen playlist that dynamically selects content based on patient and practice context |
| Info Pack | A digital, trackable document (brochure replacement) delivered via QR code; includes patient education materials |
| Consult | An interactive consultation tool delivered via the Vrtly platform; used at the point of sale within the practice |
| Content Refresh Bonus | Algorithmic boost given to brands that regularly upload new content; increases play frequency |
| Category Dominance | Upcoming premium tier offering a brand dominant SOV within its product category, limiting competitor presence |
| Vrtly Success Suite | A bundled device-sale package ($5,000/yr) co-sold with brand partners; includes 3 screens and guaranteed SOV |
| Dwell Time | Time a patient spends in the practice; cited as 45–90 min on average — the window for brand content exposure |
| ROFR | Right of First Refusal — offered to early-commit brands on Info Packs, Consults, and EMR Attribution products |

## Open Questions / Gaps

→ *flags for gap analysis or product brief clarification*

- **Hydrafacial case study sample size discrepancy**: This deck cites n=256 practices; the Q1 2025 pitch deck cites n=500. Same +83% outcome. One of these is outdated or refers to a different study window. Needs clarification before use in final documents.
- **"Sign by Mar 31" promos**: Slides 11 and 14 reference early-bird pricing tied to a March 31 deadline (2× air time, 50% off setup, $10 CPM). These are time-limited offers, not the standard product pricing. The underlying standard pricing ($5–$1/practice/mo, $20 CPM, $1,500 setup) should be treated as canonical; the promo terms should not be carried to final documents.
- **Category Dominance launch timing**: Described as "Launching in Q2" on slide 13 with no year specified. Given the deck is dated 2026, this likely means Q2 2026. Needs confirmation before treating as imminent roadmap.
- **Slide 28 is an internal working note, not a brand-facing slide**: Header reads "not a slide — this info should be included in the Demo video." The content (SmartPlaylist/Consultations/InfoPacks journey map) is useful but was flagged by the deck author as unfinished. Treat as directional context only.
- **"Vrtly Ad Manager" vs "Vrtly Ad Manager Platform"**: Used interchangeably across slides. The Q1 2025 pitch deck also uses "Vrtly Ad Manager" — should align on a single canonical name in the domain glossary.
- **Medtech Partner Program "To you (save 30%) +5%" line**: The pricing table on slide 24 is ambiguous — unclear if "+5%" means the brand pays 5% of the $6,000 value, or if there's a 30% discount applied on top of something. Needs clarification before including in final pricing docs.
