# Technical Research

## Purpose
Conduct technical feasibility and architecture-options research. Produces reports on technology choices, trade-offs, and implementation approaches for Layer 3 decisions.

## Inputs
- User research topic or technology question
- Existing Layer 3 documents for architectural context

## Outputs
- Research document in layers/layer-3-architecture/intermediate/

## Steps (TODO)
1. Clarify research scope and technology domain
2. Evaluate technology options with trade-offs
3. Assess feasibility and integration constraints
4. Synthesize recommendations
5. Write to layers/layer-3-architecture/intermediate/technical-research-<topic>.md
